iiko has been a dominant player in restaurant management software, especially in CIS and Eastern European markets. Gustri is a newer all-in-one platform built for simplicity and affordability. If you're choosing between the two — or thinking about switching — this comparison lays out the honest differences.

## Feature-by-feature comparison

### POS & ordering

**Gustri:** Clean, modern POS that works on any device with a browser. Supports dine-in, takeout, and delivery from one screen. QR ordering is built in — customers scan a code at their table and order directly from their phone.

**iiko:** Powerful POS with deep customization. Supports complex order flows, modifiers, and combos. Better suited for large, complex menus. QR ordering available but setup is more involved.

**Verdict:** iiko has more raw POS power for complex operations. Gustri is simpler to use and includes QR ordering as a first-class feature, not a bolt-on.

### Kitchen display

**Gustri:** Built-in kitchen display that shows orders in real-time on any device. Color-coded by status, with sound notifications. Works on phones and tablets — no dedicated hardware needed.

**iiko:** Kitchen display (iikoFront) is robust with routing rules, prep stations, and timing. Requires dedicated displays or terminals. More configurable but more complex to set up.

**Verdict:** iiko wins on configurability for large kitchens. Gustri wins on simplicity and zero-hardware-cost setup.

### Delivery management

**Gustri:** Built-in delivery order management with customer details, delivery fees, driver assignment, and real-time status tracking. No third-party integration needed for basic delivery.

**iiko:** Full delivery suite (iikoDelivery) with driver management, zone-based pricing, and integration with major delivery aggregators. More advanced routing and dispatch features.

**Verdict:** iiko is more powerful for delivery-heavy businesses. Gustri covers the essentials without extra setup or cost.

### Inventory & cost control

**Gustri:** Inventory tracking with stock movements, low-stock alerts, and waste logging. Expense tracking by category. Simple and effective for small operations.

**iiko:** Deep inventory with recipe costing, batch production, semi-finished products, supplier management, and automatic write-offs. This is where iiko really shines for larger operations.

**Verdict:** iiko is significantly stronger here. If detailed cost control and recipe management are critical, iiko has the edge. Gustri covers the basics well for small cafes.

### Staff management

**Gustri:** Staff scheduling with shift management and attendance tracking. Task assignment with priorities and recurring tasks. Role-based access (admin, waiter, kitchen).

**iiko:** Full HR suite with shift scheduling, time tracking, payroll integration, motivation programs, and detailed labor cost analysis.

**Verdict:** iiko offers more depth. Gustri covers what most small cafes need without the complexity.

### Analytics & reporting

**Gustri:** Dashboard with daily sales, popular items, order trends, and staff performance. Free plan shows today's data; paid plans unlock full history.

**iiko:** Comprehensive BI reporting with customizable dashboards, ABC analysis, profitability by dish, labor cost ratios, and more. One of iiko's strongest features.

**Verdict:** iiko is substantially more powerful for analytics. For small cafes that need basic insights, Gustri is sufficient.

### Multi-language support

**Gustri:** 20 languages including Japanese, English, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, and more. Menu items support multilingual names and descriptions.

**iiko:** Primarily Russian and English. Limited support for other languages.

**Verdict:** Gustri wins clearly. If you serve international customers or operate in a multilingual market, Gustri's language support is significantly better.

## Pricing comparison

### Gustri
- **Free:** 1 terminal, 50 menu items, 5 tables — includes POS, QR ordering, kitchen display, inventory
- **Standard ($29/mo):** 2 terminals, 200 items, 15 tables, 30-day analytics
- **Pro ($59/mo):** 5 terminals, unlimited items/tables, full analytics, loyalty program
- **Business ($99/mo):** Unlimited everything, multi-location, API access, phone support

### iiko
- Pricing is not publicly listed — requires contacting sales
- Typically **$50-100+/mo** depending on configuration and region
- Additional costs for implementation, training, and specific modules
- Hardware costs on top of subscription

**Verdict:** Gustri is more transparent and affordable. iiko's total cost of ownership is higher, especially when you factor in setup, hardware, and consultant fees.

## Setup & onboarding

**Gustri:** Sign up, create your menu, invite staff, start taking orders. The interface is designed so that staff can learn it in minutes without formal training. No consultant needed.

**iiko:** Typically requires professional setup. Many restaurants hire an iiko consultant for initial configuration (menu engineering, inventory setup, workflow design). Budget 1-2 weeks and $500-2,000+ for implementation.

**Verdict:** Gustri wins on speed and cost of getting started. iiko's setup complexity is justified for large operations but overkill for small cafes.

## Who should choose what

### Choose Gustri if:
- You're a small to mid-size cafe or restaurant
- You want everything in one platform without paying for each module
- You value simplicity over complex configurability
- You need multi-language support
- Budget matters — you want a generous free plan
- You want to be up and running today, not next week

### Choose iiko if:
- You're a mid to large restaurant with complex operations
- You need deep inventory with recipe costing and batch production
- Advanced analytics and labor cost analysis are critical
- You're in the CIS market and want local support infrastructure
- You have budget for professional implementation
- You need integration with major delivery aggregators

## Switching from iiko to Gustri

If you're considering the switch, here's what to expect:

1. **Menu migration** — Export your menu from iiko and recreate it in Gustri. For a typical cafe, this takes 1-2 hours.
2. **Staff onboarding** — Gustri's simpler interface means staff adapt quickly. Most learn it in a single shift.
3. **Inventory** — Start fresh with current stock counts. Gustri's inventory is simpler, so there's less to configure.
4. **No downtime** — Run both systems in parallel for a few days if you want a safety net.

## The bottom line

iiko is a powerful, mature platform built for complex restaurant operations. It earned its reputation.

Gustri is built for a different audience — small to mid-size cafes and restaurants that want a modern, affordable, all-in-one platform without the complexity and cost of enterprise software.

Neither is objectively "better." The right choice depends on your size, budget, and how much complexity you actually need. For most small cafes, Gustri delivers more value at a fraction of the cost.
